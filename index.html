About

Hi! I'm Kamil.

“Peace at home, peace in the world.”— Mustafa Kemal Atatürk

I am a graduate student (M.Sc.) in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Bilkent University and an R&D Assistant Specialist at Türk Telekom in Ankara. I completed my undergraduate studies at Middle East Technical University (METU), earning bachelor's degrees in Electrical and Electronics Engineering (2025) and Mathematics (2026) through the double major program. I am enthusiastic about mathematics, signal processing and in particular estimation theory, which is the focus of my current research.

Below you can find my academic journey and experience. My research is described on the Research page, my resume is in the CV, and a few personal favourites are on the Hobbies page.
